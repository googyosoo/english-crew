**지문:**
The common belief that practice makes perfect has been challenged by recent research. While repetition is necessary for skill development, it is not sufficient. What truly matters is the quality of practice rather than mere quantity. ① maintaining physical fitness
② **purposeful repetition**

### 요지 파악 (22번)

**지문:**
Many people believe multitasking increases productivity, but research suggests otherwise. When we switch between tasks, our brain requires time to refocus, creating what psychologists call "switching costs." These brief mental blocks can reduce overall efficiency by up to 40%. Furthermore, constant task-switching prevents deep engagement with any single activity, leading to superficial processing and increased errors. To work more effectively, we should focus on one task at a time rather than attempting to juggle multiple responsibilities simultaneously.

다음 글의 요지로 가장 적절한 것은?

① Multitasking requires special training
② Technology facilitates task management
③ **Focusing on single tasks improves efficiency**
④ Time management skills are essential
⑤ Work environments affect productivity

### 제목 파악 (24번)

**지문:**
The octopus possesses an extraordinary ability that sets it apart from most other animals: distributed intelligence. Unlike humans, whose cognition is centralized in the brain, an octopus has neurons distributed throughout its eight arms. Each arm can act semi-independently, sensing and responding to stimuli without waiting for commands from the central brain. This allows the octopus to perform multiple complex tasks simultaneously—one arm might be exploring a crevice while another manipulates an object. Scientists studying this unique nervous system hope to gain insights for developing more efficient robotic systems.

다음 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은?

① The Evolution of Marine Intelligence
② How Octopuses Hunt Their Prey
③ **Distributed Thinking: The Octopus Brain**
④ Comparing Human and Animal Cognition
⑤ Future Applications of Marine Biology

## 글의 순서 (36번)

**지문:**
**주어진 글:**
The concept of a "food mile" refers to the distance food travels from production to consumption. This metric has gained attention as consumers become more environmentally conscious.

**(A)** However, food miles alone don't tell the whole story. A product transported long distances by ship might have a lower carbon footprint than locally grown food produced in heated greenhouses.

**(B)** Therefore, when evaluating food's environmental impact, we must consider not just transportation distance but also production methods, seasonal factors, and overall energy consumption.

**(C)** Many assume that buying local food is always more sustainable because it reduces transportation emissions. This has led to the popularity of farmers' markets and local food movements.

주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?

① (A)-(C)-(B)
② (B)-(A)-(C)
③ **(C)-(A)-(B)**
④ (C)-(B)-(A)
⑤ (B)-(C)-(A)

**해설:**
- 주어진 글: food mile 개념 소개
- (C): 일반적 믿음 (로컬 푸드가 항상 지속가능)
- (A): 반론 제시 (However - 그렇지 않을 수도)
- (B): 결론 (Therefore - 종합적 고려 필요)

## 요약문 완성 (40번)

**지문:**
Ancient civilizations developed complex calendar systems to track celestial movements and predict seasonal changes. The Mayans created an intricate calendar combining solar and ritual cycles, while the Egyptians based theirs on the Nile's flooding patterns. These calendars weren't merely tools for marking time; they were deeply intertwined with religious beliefs, agricultural practices, and social organization. By observing the sky and natural phenomena, early societies could plan crops, schedule ceremonies, and maintain social order. The precision of these ancient systems reveals remarkable astronomical knowledge and demonstrates how closely human societies were connected to natural rhythms.

→ Ancient calendars, based on celestial and natural observations, served **A** functions beyond simple timekeeping, reflecting societies' **B** with natural cycles.

A                           B
① religious    ......... conflict
② **multiple**   ......... **integration**
③ agricultural  ......... disconnection
④ symbolic     ......... competition
⑤ seasonal     ......... independence

**해설:**
- A: "weren't merely tools" → multiple functions
- B: "closely connected to natural rhythms" → integration
